# PrepPilot — Objectives and Scope

## Objectives

1. Build an offline-first syllabus tracker.
2. Provide accurate topic-based progress.
3. Record study time.
4. Provide multiple timer modes.
5. Provide academic templates.
6. Support custom syllabi.
7. Provide student-controlled reminders.
8. Provide optional Gemini AI assistance.
9. Provide realistic Focus Mode.
10. Provide secure cloud synchronization.
11. Maintain clean professional UI.
12. Produce academic and production-quality documentation.

## Scope

### V1

Android, web demo, authentication, tracker, progress, timers, history, templates, notifications, offline storage, Supabase sync, Gemini AI, Focus Mode and account deletion.

### Future

More templates, advanced analytics, expanded AI, calendar integrations, iOS, desktop and wearables.
