# PrepPilot — Problem Statement

Students often use separate applications for syllabus tracking, timers, reminders, study history and AI assistance.

This creates a fragmented workflow.

Students may not have a clear view of:

- Which topics are complete
- Which chapters remain
- How much of a subject is complete
- How much time they have actually studied

PrepPilot combines syllabus tracking, progress visualization, study timing, history, templates, reminders and optional AI assistance in one application.

The core tracker remains functional without internet connectivity.
